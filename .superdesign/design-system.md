# Avel aviation marketing site design system

## Brand

Avel is a private aviation operations workspace. Use YD Gothic 130 Pro for the marketing site. Use the supplied Avel wordmark assets exactly. Keep black and white mark variants on their matching surfaces. Do not substitute text, initials, emoji, or invented brand marks.

## Palette

- Primary black: `#050505`
- Near black: `#101112`
- Bright silver: `#D9DDE1`
- Mid silver: `#A8ADB3`
- Soft silver: `#F0F1F2`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Text on light: `#141518`
- Text on dark: `#F8F9FA`
- Dividers: neutral silver with clear contrast

No blue or cyan accents. The hero uses a clean black, silver, and white gradient that slowly shifts and pools across the background. Use narrow silver glints for highlights, never a low-contrast button fill.

## Interface and icon language

- Preserve the current marketing page section order and overall desktop composition.
- Product recreations use the supplied Avel application screenshots as the structural reference: fixed left navigation, white central panel, light gray outer surface, clear separators, and a right assistant panel where shown.
- Buttons already present in the landing-page structure stay in their current locations and count. Use fully solid black or white fills with readable text. Add no new landing-page buttons or calls to action.
- Use the Lucide outline icon library, matching the supplied Avel sidebar screenshot. Do not draw, invent, or hand-author icons. Do not use emoji, Unicode symbols as icons, Iconify, or custom SVG icon paths.
- Hero and section motion should feel weighty, smooth, and deliberate. Use long easing and restrained reveal timing. Keep scrolling native and do not hijack it. Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Use crisp borders, substantial black and white fields, and restrained silver highlights. Avoid faded or translucent button states.

## Product content rules

- Show Avel One, Sales and Customer Revenue, Empty Legs and Network Revenue, Trip Operations, setup topology, shared inbox, charter requests, brokerage, fleet, trips, and integrations.
- The moving globe and route trail is a designed sample network unless validated live-flight data is shown. Clearly label it as sample or simulated.
- Do not create customer quotes, performance metrics, live integration claims, or a confirmed Avel and Stratos partnership. Use neutral operator or partner placeholders until approved.
- Use no em dash characters in user-facing content.
