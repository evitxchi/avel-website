# Routes

The site has no client router or framework routing.

| URL | Entry | Page |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | `index.html` | Hamilton private aviation marketing page |

## In-page anchors

- `#home`: hero and product preview
- `#stories`: customer stories
- `#lead-system`: unified lead system
- `#calculator`: revenue calculator
- `#modules`: product modules
- `#results`: results and testimonials
- `#footer`: footer and final CTA

Navigation, forms, dialogs, and scroll state are wired by `main.js`.
